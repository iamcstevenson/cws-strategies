How we work

From first call to handoff

Every project follows the same four steps, so you always know what's next.

  1. Talk it through

    A conversation about your organization, your team, and what's slowing you down.

  2. Plan and design

    A clear plan and a design you can react to before anything gets built.

  3. Build and test

    We build it, test it on real devices, and walk you through it as it comes together.

  4. Launch and hand off

    We launch and train your team. Then you choose: keep us on, or take the reins.
